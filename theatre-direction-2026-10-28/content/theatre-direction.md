# Corbie: the theatre, the work, and the welcome

Draft exploration, 28 October 2026. Incorporates Callie’s theatre direction and the subsequent suggestion that the sky sequence should reveal the portals. This is an experience proposal, not approved website copy, a revised sitemap, or a deployed interaction.

## The central idea

Corbie’s world can borrow the experience of a well-run theatre: the welcome before the performance, the anticipation of a lit space, the concentration of attention on the work, the many contributions that sustain it, and the return to daylight carrying something new.

The ornate frame supports the art. It does not compete with it. Comfort and care are expressed through a usable website: clear orientation, readable words, working controls, predictable forms, and generous space. The people maintaining the space matter as much as its most visible performers. Cleaning, maintenance, preparation, access, hospitality, scheduling, sound, lighting, scenery, and performance are all part of the experience. This is a model for valuing interdependent work, not a claim that Corbie currently employs all those roles or operates a theatre.

The actual clients and creatives do not map to separate social classes within this metaphor. Both can be participants, makers, collaborators, and an audience. Nobody is directed to a secondary service entrance.

## Recommendation: the sky sequence reveals Home’s two portals

Separate the visual dream sequence from the page named The Dream. The opening belongs to Home’s shared arrival. The Dream page then expands the community’s purpose and possibilities. A client can enter Companies directly without first reading the community manifesto.

1. **Arrive.** Corbie’s actual mark, a clear heading, and two live labeled links are immediately available on solid ground. A finished painted landscape is present; clouds never act as a loading screen.
2. **Anticipate.** Two cloud banks sit across the upper scene, partly veiling its architectural context. Their separation reveals the scene’s depth. They never cover either route’s label, focus indicator, or click target.
3. **Reveal.** The sun peeks from behind a cloud. The left stone entrance and right garden stair are now visible as two distinct destinations within the same world. Their reveal happens together, with equal scale and light.
4. **Release.** A few birds cross the shared gap once. They do not point toward one preferred door or turn into the logo. The scene settles.
5. **Enter.** For clients -> Companies. For community -> The Dream, then Creatives for practical participation. Each destination can have its own short visual transition, but navigation never waits for playback to finish.

An exploratory production target is roughly three seconds, subject to testing. Retain a visible Skip opening control. Direct page visits should not be redirected through an introduction. Do not replay an entrance on every navigation or on Back. With reduced motion, show the completed two-portal scene immediately.

### What happens to the clock?

Keep the small day/night dial as a secondary Dream chapter device. Do not make visitors understand a clock before they can choose a portal. Home can carry the sun/cloud motif without running a full day-to-night cycle before the entrances appear.

On Dream, the dial can accompany the shift from what exists now to what could grow. The text must carry that distinction explicitly. The dial is not a countdown or promised roadmap. The cinematic nighttime treatment is strongest at a considered reading pause, rather than as a compulsory opening delay.

## Dream first: an atmospheric auditorium

**Emotional purpose:** the ceiling seems to open beyond the building, while the room remains a comfortable place to gather.

**Visual proposal:** a restrained proscenium edge, a painted landscape as the stage world, a symbolic sky above it, and a small celestial dial outside the reading measure. The existing Palmer landscape can remain the initial study. Rebuild any final painted cutouts from intact source files.

**Sequence within the page:**

- A short shared arrival cue recalls Home, without replaying the whole opening.
- The invitation appears immediately. One slow cloud separation can be optional.
- The present-day community description sits on a clear, light reading surface.
- A short dusk/night illustration accompanies future possibility. Keep current versus future language explicit.
- The final section returns toward Gesso with an actionable invitation and links to practical participation.

**People behind the feeling:** the person who made the place welcoming, the people who prepared and maintained it, and the collaborators who make a shared experience possible. Honor those contributions through specific real examples when available, not a fictitious cast list.

**Still version:** one finished landscape with both light and depth, an unobtrusive dial, and all content available. No cloud needs to move for the invitation to make sense.

## Proof: the most valuable theatrical interaction

A proposed case-study structure:

1. The visible result: a real cleared example and the useful change it made.
2. The situation before it: what was not working and for whom.
3. The system beneath it: briefing, production, feedback, coordination, standards, delivery, access and maintenance, only where actually relevant.
4. The people and contribution: who did what, including enabling and supporting work, without implying Callie made everyone else’s contribution.
5. What can be established: observed outcome, evidence, uncertainty and constraints.

An optional layer view can connect these parts visually. It should illuminate real interdependence rather than stage a fake reveal of a “secret process.” This is a useful bridge for non-designers: a finished image is only one visible part of the work.

## The daylight exit

Use Callie’s dark-theatre-to-daylight memory at meaningful transitions, not as a flash effect on every page. A promising place is the move from Dream’s atmospheric scene to the light, practical Join page, or from a rich Proof story to the clear Share form.

The source page retains its local palette until navigation. The destination opens directly on a comfortable Gesso surface, optionally with a brief opacity transition that never prevents reading or interaction. No pure-white flash, simulated pupil adjustment, forced scroll, or lengthy exposure animation. The browser Back action remains ordinary.

The feeling is release, perspective and renewed attention to the real world. The site should help someone return to a useful action carrying the story, not keep them trapped inside the scenery.

## Material and ornament rules to explore

- The proscenium frames selected feature scenes. It is not a border around every paragraph.
- Use depth at the edges, a quiet centre, and one deliberate focal emphasis.
- Pick a coherent construction vocabulary: shallow plaster relief, fine metalwork, aged paint, fabric folds, and worn material at an appropriate scale. Select a few rather than assembling every theatre style.
- Comfort comes from space, sight lines, stable controls and legibility. It is not an excuse for expensive upholstery imagery on every page.
- The authentic paintings supply the changing stage world. The modern logo and type remain consistent orientation.
- Sound is not required. No ambient audio without a deliberate user action and clear control.
- Preserve real-world context. A beautiful historic photograph is not automatically public domain, and a public-domain object is not automatically appropriate decoration.
- Avoid nostalgia that romanticizes exclusion, hierarchy, minstrel traditions, or unequal access. Borrow hospitality, skilled construction and immersion; build a welcome that is open now.

2. **Then:** refine one Dream scene with a restrained architectural frame and optional dial, keeping the chapter text available.
3. **Then:** prototype one truthful Proof layer view using an actual Corbie build example. This carries the human/operational idea beyond decoration.
4. **Finally:** demonstrate the daylight arrival into Join or Share and carry the same care into the forms.

For the next launch, a strong finished still, two clear entrances, real content and usable forms take priority over multiple elaborate scene changes. No TODO item is marked complete by this exploration.
