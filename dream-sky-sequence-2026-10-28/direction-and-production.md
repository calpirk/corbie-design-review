# Dream: clouds, birds and a day/night chapter dial

Draft direction, 28 October 2026. User preference: establish more whimsy through stylized clouds, sun, moon and stars alongside authentic paintings and modern logos/type.

## Sequence

1. Community portal -> Dream: keep a matching landscape edge. Title, navigation and skip link are available immediately.
2. Clouds: two foreground banks part laterally with slight depth differences. Avoid a black or empty loading phase.
3. Sun: reveal a small radial sun behind the cloud edge. The sun becomes visible through occlusion, not a huge invented lens flare.
4. Birds: a small flock crosses once in a loose stagger. No repeated swarm, looping birds beside reading text, or transformation of the brand mark.
5. Day/night: a traditional small dial rotates a celestial disk behind an aperture. The sun leaves as moon and stars arrive. Let the sky settle while the invitation remains readable.

The interactive study uses a deliberately slow 14-second, user-started playback for review. Production target: roughly 3-4 seconds on entry, or a short optional scroll progression. No repeated autoplay. Do not implement mandatory time or scroll gates.

## Art direction

Three layers of visual language: authentic paintings for the world and human/material richness; a sparse family of original ornamental sky symbols for imagination; the approved geometric identity and contemporary typography for orientation. Slightly engraved line work and restrained flat colors suit the dial. Avoid copying the watch’s proprietary arrangement or using watch photographs as Corbie art.

One small facial detail on sun/moon is an option, not a rule for every object. Roman numerals and concentric rings signal a traditional clock. Keep all actual section names and controls in contemporary live text. The clock is a chapter guide, not a countdown, fake scarcity device or promised community roadmap.

## Recommended placement

Small chapter dial beside Dream’s introduction, then section links in normal document flow. Its state may progress from current community to future possibility, but the text must distinguish what exists today and what remains a dream. A factual Lore timeline is a separate possible application requiring approved dates and milestones.

## Production layers

- 00-source-records: full original, metadata, rights, object link and derivative notes.
- 10-painted-background: The Golden Hour as a source-faithful Smart Object; retain the full original. Select the landscape crop deliberately.
- 20-sky: separate day and night grounds. A night overlay is a modern treatment, not an original historical night painting.
- 30-celestial: original vector sun, crescent moon, stars, optional sparse face lines. No new logo.
- 40-clouds: left front cloud, right front cloud and optional small rear curl. Native vector forms are placeholders for a refined hand-drawn or sourced family.
- 50-birds: separate bird silhouettes/poses, no more detail than the chosen scale supports. The prototype’s simple wing curves are schematic movement placeholders, not finished crow artwork or historical cutouts.
- 60-dial: unchanged outer ring/numerals, aperture mask, rotating sky disk, separate hand. Keep decorative numerals out of accessible navigation.
- 90-live-content: modern heading, readable copy, buttons, chapter controls, skip route, logo. Never bake these into the scene export.

Suggested desktop art canvas: 2800 x 1800 with generous crop allowance. Phone: recompose to a short wide opening with two cloud banks and one celestial focal point; put the chapter dial below the heading rather than shrinking a desktop clock into an unreadable corner. Keep first useful content visible quickly.

## Access and performance

No autoplay in the review. Keyboard-operable range and stage buttons. Skip goes to content. Respect reduced motion: open to a finished lit still and advance between stills only. Content and links are in ordinary document order and do not depend on animation. Pause user-started playback when the tab is hidden. Production should use the already approved GSAP/ScrollTrigger stack, transform/opacity where possible, and load optional motion after useful content is visible. This prototype is standalone native SVG/JavaScript and adds no project dependency.
