# Corbie searchable theatre and artwork catalog

Open index.html locally. No server, account or network request is required for the 81 image previews, search or filtering. Source links and full downloads open the relevant institutions online. Fonts, previews and metadata are bundled.

## Scope

- 21 newly downloaded and visually reviewed theatre-related artworks from the Met and Cleveland, with individual public-domain/CC0 API records.
- 60 deduplicated source images carried forward from the earlier 27-image and 34-image research sets. Source IDs, institutions and earlier rights distinctions are retained.
- Nine separate reference/unresolved records: four Library of Congress Federal Theatre photographs, two Smithsonian research leads, two architectural-photo references from Callie’s AD article, and the watch reference. These are not counted among visually reviewed reusable images and have no bundled image preview. Their records remain useful leads rather than claimed public-domain assets.

All inspected source images remain in the catalog, including Holds, Passes and earlier alternates. Search engine hits that were never selected for image inspection are not counted as reviewed. Generated collage drafts, private drive documents, unknown drive-image contact sheets and personal files are excluded.

## Use

Search across titles, artists, institutions, media, tags, suggested pages and notes. Combine collection, decision, rights, page and visual-element filters. Use Theatre shortlist to see the strongest new candidates; reset to search the whole source history. Native browser Find also works if JavaScript is off.

Each card has an image anchor and Copy image link. Filter settings are recorded in the URL query. To share a local catalog with someone on another computer, send the ZIP and have them extract it, open index.html, then use the named image or stable source ID. A local file path is not a publicly hosted link.

## Rights and evidence

Visual suitability and rights status are separate filters. Shortlist is a design recommendation, not publication approval. Museum CC0/public-domain records, Wellcome Public Domain Mark, NASA media-use conditions and PDR archive rights declarations are kept separate. A historic subject does not automatically make a modern photograph public domain. Individual image reuse conditions still matter.

LOC’s search API, several resource pages and direct exhibit image downloads returned 403 in this run. The official exhibit records were readable, so the relevant human-labor leads remain in the catalog with unresolved item rights and no visual-review claim. The two Smithsonian leads were identified from official indexed records but their images were not retrieved. No institution access block was interpreted as clearance.

All new theatre images were reviewed on contact sheets. Suggested uses, extraction notes and reasons to hold/pass are original curation. Particularly useful sources: met-401799 (proscenium), met-344485 (front and side construction), met-377854 and met-335795 (stage depth), met-700468 (theatrical sky), met-700512 (stage machinery), cma-95401 (theatre/audience relationship). Keep original cultural and production context accurate.

## Files

catalog.json and catalog.csv are the portable structured records. records/ holds source API metadata where available; previews/ contains display derivatives. Full/native museum download links are recorded; high-resolution masters are not duplicated in this review package. No website, brand standard, Cof entry or deployment has been changed.
