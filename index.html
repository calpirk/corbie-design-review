A small door to somewhere else

New reference from Callie: the door dial in Howl’s Moving Castle, explored through Dianne Hradsky’s “Howl’s Moving Castle and The Door to Liminality” (2018).

Imagine the wayfinding caret as a small pointer on an original Corbie dial. Open “Explore Corbie” to see labeled destinations; choose a link and the pointer turns toward that part of the world. The two client and community entrances stay clear and equal.

The Easter egg lives in the gesture of choosing what lies beyond a threshold. The exact mechanism is still a proposal: original Corbie ornament, ordinary links, keyboard and touch support, and a finished still for reduced motion. No recognition of the film is required.

This is a linked inspiration reference, not a public-domain image source. No film stills or copied dial artwork are included. The day/night clock has a separate storytelling role.
